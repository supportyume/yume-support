Community Guidelines

Our Zero Tolerance Policy

YUME is a safe space for meaningful connections. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. Users who violate our community standards will be permanently banned without warning.

Prohibited Content

The following are strictly prohibited on YUME:

  • Nudity or sexually explicit content
  • Hate speech or discrimination of any kind
  • Harassment, bullying, or threats against other users
  • Violence, gore, or promotion of illegal acts
  • Commercial spam or fraudulent activities

Getting Started

What is Guest Mode?

Guest Mode allows you to explore YUME immediately without creating an account. You can chat and discover people nearby for a 14-day trial period.

How do I save my data?

To keep your friends and chat history forever, you must "Upgrade" your guest account to a full account using your Email or Google Sign-In before the 14-day trial ends. Look for the upgrade banner on your Home or Settings screen.

Social & Discovery

How does the "Distance" feature work?

YUME uses your device's location to calculate the approximate distance between you and other dreamers. Your exact coordinates are never shared with others—only the distance and your general city.

Can I hide my location?

Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy and toggle "Show my location" off. You can also hide your Online Status in the same menu.

Reporting & Safety Tools

How do I report a Post?

If you see offensive content on the Timeline, tap the three-dot (...) menu at the top right of the post card and select "Report Post". Select the reason for your report and submit.

How do I report or block a User?

In a Chat or on a User's Profile, tap the three-dot (...) menu and select "Report" or "Block". Blocking a user will immediately remove them from your friends list and hide them from your discovery feed.

What happens after I report something?

Our moderation team reviews reports 24/7. Objectionable content is removed within 24 hours, and accounts found in violation of our guidelines are suspended or banned.

Account Management

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Danger Zone and select "Delete Account". For your protection, your account will be scheduled for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days. You can cancel this request by logging back in within the 30-day window.
