Getting Started

What is Guest Mode?

Guest Mode allows you to explore YUME immediately without creating an account. You can chat and discover people nearby for up to 14 days.

How do I save my data?

To keep your friends and chat history forever, you must "Upgrade" your guest account to a full account using your Email or Google Sign-In before the 14-day trial ends.

Social & Discovery

How does the "Distance" feature work?

YUME uses your GPS location to calculate how far away other users are. This is updated in real-time while you use the app.

Can I hide my location?

Yes. Go to Settings > Privacy and toggle "Show my location" off. You will still be able to use the app, but other users won't see your distance or city.

Safety & Privacy

How do I report someone?

In any chat room, tap the three dots (menu) at the top right and select "Report". Our moderation team reviews every report within 24 hours.

What happens when I block a user?

Blocking is immediate. The user will be removed from your friends list, they cannot message you, and they will no longer appear in your Discovery feed.

Account Management

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Danger Zone and select "Delete Account". For security, your account will be scheduled for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days. This action cannot be undone.
