Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to YUME Chat. Your privacy is critical to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and support website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Profile Information: Name, age, gender, interests, and profile photo provided during registration or guest account setup.
  • Location Data: Approximate location (city/country level) used for "Nearby Discovery" features. Your precise GPS coordinates are processed locally and only approximate distances are shared.
  • Device Information: FCM tokens for push notifications and device model information for technical support.
  • Authentication: Email addresses or Google/Apple IDs used for account verification.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the YUME social experience.
  • To enable features like "Nearby Discovery" and real-time chat.
  • To enforce our Community Guidelines and ensure user safety.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

YUME does not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Information you choose to make public (name, bio, interests, distance).
  • For legal reasons: To meet any applicable law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (by Google) for database, storage, and authentication services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Account Deletion: You can request account deletion at any time via Settings > Danger Zone.
  • Grace Period: Data is scheduled for permanent removal 30 days after the request.
  • Total Wipe: Upon final deletion, all Auth records, Firestore documents, and Storage files (images) are permanently erased from our production servers.

6. Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data, including App Check for API protection and Firebase Security Rules for data access control.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at supportyume232@gmail.com.
