Zero Tolerance Policy

YUME Chat maintains a Zero Tolerance Policy regarding objectionable content or abusive behavior. This is necessary to maintain a safe and welcoming environment for all users.

Users who violate these terms will be permanently banned from the service immediately and without prior warning.

1. User Responsibilities

By using YUME Chat, you agree to:

  • Be at least 18 years of age.
  • Provide accurate profile information.
  • Interact with other users respectfully and lawfully.
  • Maintain the security of your authentication credentials.

2. Community Guidelines

The following activities and content types are strictly prohibited:

  • Objectionable Content: Nudity, sexually explicit content, or excessive violence.
  • Abusive Behavior: Harassment, bullying, stalking, or making threats.
  • Discrimination: Hate speech or discrimination based on race, religion, sexual orientation, or gender identity.
  • Fraud: Impersonation of others or engaging in fraudulent financial activities.
  • Spam: Sending unsolicited commercial messages or links.

3. Content Moderation & Reporting

YUME uses a hybrid human-and-automated moderation system to keep the community safe:

  • Reporting: Users can report any objectionable content or abusive users via the three-dot (...) menu found in the Timeline and Profile screens.
  • Automated Hiding: Any post that receives 2 unique community reports is automatically hidden from the public feed pending further review.
  • User Suspension: Users receiving multiple valid reports will be automatically suspended by our moderation engine.

4. Account Management

  • Deletion: Users may delete their account at any time via Settings.
  • Persistence: Following a deletion request, data is permanently removed after a 30-day grace period.

5. Limitation of Liability

YUME Chat is provided "as is." We are not liable for any damages arising from your use of the service or interactions with other users. While we strive to moderate content aggressively, you use the service at your own risk.
